Full-Spectrum Computational Chemistry Capabilities

Everything required for modern virtual screening, integrated into a high-performance web architecture.

Automated Receptor & Ligand Prep

Automatic RCSB PDB download, water removal, pH 7.4 polar hydrogen addition, Gasteiger charges, 3D conformer embedding, and energy minimization.

Smart Grid-Box Management

Calculates bounding boxes automatically around co-crystallized native ligands, active-site amino acid residues, or custom spatial coordinates.

Parallel Multi-Core Engine

Distributes virtual screening across multi-core CPU threads simultaneously with real-time progress streaming updates to your web dashboard.

Interaction Profiling & LE Ranking

Calculates Ligand Efficiency ($\text{LE} = \Delta G / N_{\text{heavy}}$), non-covalent hydrogen bonding, hydrophobic contacts, and binding pose rank order.

Self-Docking RMSD Validation

Automated native ligand re-docking validation computing heavy-atom RMSD ($\text{RMSD} \le 2.0\text{ \AA}$) to verify grid box accuracy before screening novel libraries.

Publication Deliverables & Audits

Exports structured CSV, Excel tables, publication-ready PDF reports, PyMOL zip bundles, and 100% reproducible JSON audit logs.
